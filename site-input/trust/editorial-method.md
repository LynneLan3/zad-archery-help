# Editorial method

Guide pages on this starter site may draw on:

- official announcements, store pages, or documentation
- firsthand gameplay notes and screenshots we can describe accurately
- community research that we re-check before publishing
- manual review before a page is linked from the Hub
- follow-up edits when facts change or better evidence appears

This is starter copy for the template. It does not describe a dedicated editorial desk or legal review team that does not exist for your site.
