## Verified facts used by the V4 input

- Title: **Sucker for Love: Crush Landing**.
- Developer: **Akabaka**; publisher: **Black Lantern Collective**.
- Steam lists the release date as **August 31, 2026**.
- Steam describes it as a horror-comedy visual novel in which the sentient comet Hheily crash-lands in the protagonist's apartment; the player is asked to feed her flame.
- The official feature list includes multiple endings, fully voiced dialogue, hand-crafted music and artwork, and 360-degree hand-drawn 2D apartment environments.
- Steam lists English interface, full audio, and subtitles; single-player, Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, and Family Sharing; and a minimum of Windows 10 and 4 GB RAM.
- The developer's mature-content notice says the game includes adult themes and some comically disturbing body horror, while not featuring explicit content.
