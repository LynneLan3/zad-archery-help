# Zad Archery Beginner Guide: Current Progression After the Mega Update

Quick Answer
After the September 4 Mega Update, a current Zad Archery run moves from early wave clearing into Jobs and Preferred Job, then Symbols, then Portal Currency and Rings after level 30, and finally Endless once the Level 50 cap is gone. Learn each system as it unlocks; do not treat unverified Symbol or Ring rankings as required beginner advice.

How progression flows (one-run view)
1. Start by clearing early waves, picking up loot, and using the skill tree to invest basic upgrades.
2. Move into Jobs as your main growth axis; choose and advance jobs while watching for the new Preferred Job option.
3. After job progression is established, add Symbols (including Exalted Symbols) as the next layer of growth.
4. After level 30, Portal Currency becomes relevant — it feeds the Ring System (9 Ring perks).
5. Once the Level 50 cap was removed, Endless content becomes the late-stage farming option.

Core loop (what you actually do)
- Clear target-like enemies and waves.
- Pick up loot and resources.
- Reinvest loot into upgrades, skills, crafting, and job progression.
This basic loop remains the game’s central player activity.

Steam tags (what the store lists)
Steam tags include: Incremental, Idler, Loot, Platformer, Roguelite, RPG, Strategy, Crafting and Mining.

Early progression: wave clearing → loot → systems
- Early runs still focus on wave clearing, taking loot, and spending points in the skill tree.
- Early unlocks include crafting and mining systems; these remain part of the first-stage progression.

Jobs and Preferred Job
- Jobs remain a core growth axis in every run.
- The September 4 Mega Update adds Preferred Job: when you set a Preferred Job it doubles all bonuses of that preferred job.
- Treat Preferred Job selection as a major choice — it amplifies the job’s existing bonuses rather than replacing job progression itself.

Symbol System (post-job growth layer)
- The Symbol System is a post-job progression layer added by the Mega Update.
- It includes 50+ Symbols and Exalted Symbols as new upgrade options.
- Official notes confirm the system and counts, but the developers do not publish a verified best-Symbol ranking or exact hidden values. Do not treat unverified rankings as required beginner advice.

Portal Currency and the Ring System (after level 30)
- After level 30, Portal Currency becomes relevant and is invested into the Ring System.
- The Ring System includes 9 Ring perks.
- Official notes confirm the existence of the Ring System and that Portal Currency feeds it; they do not publish a complete Ring ranking or exact hidden values.

Endless and the level cap change
- The Mega Update removed the Level 50 cap.
- Endless farming is now available as a late-stage option once that cap is gone.
- Do not assume official documentation of optimal Endless routes; developers have not published verified farming routes or exact hidden values.

Patch status and notable fixes
- The Mega Update was followed by September 5 Patch 1.1.1 (current follow-up patch).
- Patch 1.1.1 includes several fixes and quality-of-life changes, among them:
  - Guardian / skipped-level recovery fixes (missed Guardian can appear next level; if already passed, Guardian counted defeated and currency granted).
  - Fixes to the skipped-level bug that could lose Portal Currency (corrected on load).
  - Mining ore auto-collect when too much ore piles up.
  - Effects Amount slider and some UI/currency wrap fixes.
  - Leftover skill effects no longer kill the next Run Portal.
These are the developer-acknowledged changes in the follow-up patch.

Common mistakes to avoid
- Treating unverified Symbol or Ring rankings as required or definitive guidance.
- Ignoring Preferred Job — it doubles job bonuses and meaningfully affects scaling.
- Assuming Endless is available before you reach late-stage play; Endless is relevant after the Level 50 cap removal.
- Overlooking Portal Currency once you pass level 30 — it’s the gateway to Ring perks.
- Expecting exact hidden values or complete official rankings — developers have not published these.

What happens next (practical next steps)
- While leveling, prioritize wave clearing, loot pickup, and basic skill-tree investments.
- When jobs unlock, pick and progress jobs; consider which job you may set as Preferred Job later.
- After job progression feels steady, start unlocking and investing in Symbols (including Exalted Symbols).
- At level 30+, collect and save Portal Currency to invest in Rings.
- After you clear into late-stage play, try Endless content for long-term farming now that the Level 50 cap is removed.

Official confirmation and limits
- Public official notes confirm the presence of the new systems (Preferred Job, Symbol System, Ring System, Endless) and concrete counts where stated (50+ Symbols, 9 Ring perks).
- Official notes do not publish a verified best-Symbol ranking, complete Ring ranking, or exact hidden values; treat those as unknown or community-sourced, not official.

FAQ
Q: What does Preferred Job do?
A: Preferred Job (added in the September 4 Mega Update) doubles all bonuses of the preferred job. Jobs remain a core growth axis.

Q: How many Symbols are in the Symbol System?
A: The Symbol System contains 50+ Symbols plus Exalted Symbols, per the Mega Update notes.

Q: When do Rings and Portal Currency unlock?
A: Portal Currency becomes relevant after level 30 and feeds the Ring System, which includes 9 Ring perks.

Q: Is Endless available now?
A: Yes — Endless farming is available after the Level 50 cap was removed in the Mega Update.

Q: What is the current patch after the Mega Update?
A: The current follow-up patch is September 5 Patch 1.1.1, which includes Guardian and skipped-level recovery fixes and other improvements listed in official notes.
