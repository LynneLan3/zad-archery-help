# How Long Is Zad Archery Now? Launch Run vs Post-Mega Update

Quick answer
Do not treat the current full game as a fixed 4–5 hour finish. Launch main progression was designed around about five hours, and launch-day players often finished or maxed major systems in roughly 4–5.5 hours. After the September 4 Mega Update, the Level 50 cap is removed and Endless farming exists, so the current endpoint is open-ended beyond that launch main run.

What the developer said before launch
- Samharia Studios said the game was designed to take around five hours in the main progression.
- The studio also noted multiple major content systems and a set of achievements at release, and that build choices and loot can change completion time.

What players reported at launch
1. Community reports from launch-day players put finishing or maxing major systems at roughly 4 to 5.5 hours.
2. Treat these reports as evidence of the launch main-progression run time, not as a full-game endpoint after later updates.

What changed in the September 4 Mega Update
- The September 4 Mega Update removed the Level 50 cap.
- Endless farming was added in the same update.
- Because post-50 and Endless content now exist, the game should not be described as ending at a fixed 4–5 hour cap.

Factors the developer highlighted that affect run time
- Build choices and loot can increase or decrease how long a run takes.
- Pursuing 100% achievements will extend total playtime compared with a straight main-progression run.

What this means for expected playtime now
- If you want a launch-style main run, existing launch-day reports (~4–5.5 hours) reflect that experience.
- If you include post-50 progression or Endless farming added by the Mega Update, playtime is open-ended and no fixed full-game finish time applies.
- There is not yet a verified average clear time for post-50 or Endless endpoints in the available recovery research; averages for those endpoints are unknown at this time.

Common mistakes
- Treating the 4–5.5 hour launch reports as the current, complete-game length after the Mega Update.
- Assuming the Level 50 cap still defines an endpoint (the cap was removed).
- Expecting a verified average clear time for post-50 or Endless content when none has been established yet.

What happens next
- Playtime for players who pursue post-50 or Endless content will vary; community and developer reports will be the best source for updated average times as data accumulates.
- For a quick, launch-style playthrough, use the launch-day 4–5.5 hour reports as a reference for main progression only.

FAQ

A: No. The game’s launch main progression was designed around five hours and many launch players finished or maxed major systems in roughly 4–5.5 hours, but the September 4 Mega Update removed the Level 50 cap and added Endless farming—so the current full game is not a fixed 4–5 hour finish.

Q: How long did players take at launch?
A: Launch-day player reports clustered around 4 to 5.5 hours to finish or max major systems; treat that as launch main-progression evidence.

A: Yes. The developer noted that build choices and loot affect duration, and that aiming for 100% achievements will increase total time.

A: Not yet. There is not a verified average clear time for post-50 or Endless endpoints in the available research.
