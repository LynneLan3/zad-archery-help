# Zad Archery Patch 1.0.2: Historical Launch Fixes (Not Current)

Quick Answer
Patch 1.0.2 is a launch-week historical patch, not the current version. It fixed monster gem counting below your highest level, restored missed Guardians that could lock the King, and repaired the Defeat the King achievement after Steam connectivity problems. For the current game, start with the September 4 Mega Update and September 5 Patch 1.1.1.

## Status (historical)
- Patch 1.0.2 is retained as a historical launch-week patch and should not be treated as the live current patch.
- For the latest changes and fixes, follow the September 4 Mega Update first, then the September 5 Patch 1.1.1.

## What 1.0.2 changed (verified)
1. Monster gem counting
   - Monster gems are counted properly when farming a level below your highest level.
2. Missed Guardian restoration
   - A Guardian that failed to appear could permanently lock the King; missed Guardians were restored on restart to prevent that lock.
3. Achievement fix
   - The Defeat the King achievement unlock was repaired for cases where Steam was unreachable.
4. Settings change
   - The player's own screen resolution was added as the first option in settings.

## How this patch fits into post-launch updates
- Patch 1.0.2 is a launch-week fix set. It is not the current patch.
- To view or apply current-version changes, review the September 4 Mega Update, then the September 5 Patch 1.1.1 in that order.

## Common mistakes
- Assuming Patch 1.0.2 is the current patch. It is historical evidence of early launch fixes.
- Skipping the September 4 and September 5 updates when looking for the latest fixes or systems.
