# Zad Archery Symbols: What the Mega Update System Adds

The September 4 Mega Update adds a Symbol System with 50+ Symbols and Exalted Symbols. This page covers only that verified system fact. It does not rank best Symbols or publish unverified hidden values.

## What the update added (verified)

- A new Symbol System was added in the September 4 Mega Update.
- Official notes describe 50+ Symbols and the presence of Exalted Symbols.
- Symbols are part of progression and sit after Jobs / the Preferred Job step and before later Portal Currency / Rings progression.

## Where Symbols sit in progression

In the current verified progression ordering:
1. Jobs / Preferred Job — the Preferred Job exists as part of progression.
2. Symbols — the Symbol System (50+ Symbols, including Exalted Symbols) comes after Jobs/Preferred Job.
3. Later progression — Portal Currency and the Ring progression follow Symbols.

This page documents those placement facts only; it does not expand on later Portal or Ring mechanics.
